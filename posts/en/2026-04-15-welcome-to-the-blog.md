# Welcome!

This blog opens a new section on insight91.be. Our goal: regularly share **concrete cases**, **lessons learned** and **reflections** around the three areas that drive us — data, ERP and applied AI.

## Why a blog?

Three reasons:

1. **Transparency.** Describing what we do, how and why, is the best way for you to judge whether we match your needs.
2. **Sharing.** Many problems repeat from one project to the next. If our articles can spare other teams from repeating our mistakes, all the better.
3. **Visibility.** Honestly: an active blog also helps SEO. It's a long-term investment.

## What to expect

A few planned formats:

- **Anonymised case studies** (with client agreement) on Odoo deployments, data projects, and AI experiments.
- **Technical deep dives** on focused topics (modelling, tooling choices, common pitfalls).
- **Market trends**: what's changing, what's staying, what deserves caution.

More posts coming in the following weeks. Meanwhile, feel free to [contact us](/en/contact) to discuss your project.
